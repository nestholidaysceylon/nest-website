About Us

Introduction

More Than a
Destination

At Nest Holidays Ceylon, we believe travel is more than just visiting a destination — it's about creating unforgettable experiences, meaningful connections, and lifelong memories.


We are a Sri Lanka-focused travel brand powered by our parent company, Nest Tours Ltd, dedicated to providing personalised holiday experiences for travellers from the UK and around the world.

Who We Are

Our Story

Nest Holidays Ceylon was created with one simple goal: to bring the beauty, culture, and warmth of Sri Lanka closer to you.

With a strong foundation in the travel industry and deep local knowledge, our team combines international service standards with authentic Sri Lankan hospitality. Whether you're planning a relaxing beach escape, a cultural journey, or a luxury getaway, we are here to make your travel seamless and stress-free.

Why Sri Lanka?

A Destination
Like No Other

Sri Lanka is a destination like no other — a tropical island filled with:

Golden Beaches

Miles of pristine coastline lapped by warm Indian Ocean waters.

Ancient Heritage Sites

Nine UNESCO World Heritage Sites, some dating back to the 3rd century BC.

Lush Tea Plantations

Misty hill country estates producing the world-famous Ceylon Tea.

Wildlife Safaris

Leopards, elephants, and exotic birds in 14 protected national parks.

Rich Culture & Cuisine

A vibrant blend of traditions, festivals, and unforgettable flavours.

At Nest Holidays Ceylon, we don't just sell holidays — we help you experience Sri Lanka the right way.
